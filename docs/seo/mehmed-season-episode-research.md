# Sultan Muhammad Fateh / Mehmed season and episode search research

Researched 3 October 2026 using OpenRush and public search results. This supersedes the earlier brief's statement that no keyword-volume evidence is available. No website code or live data was changed.

## Scope and interpretation

Twelve successful keyword-expansion calls covered all four season seeds in Pakistan (Roman-script queries, English language setting), all four original-title season seeds in the US, episode queries, dubbed queries, English-subtitle queries and a UK original-title query. The CSV contains 308 distinct market/keyword rows, including irrelevant suggestions preserved for transparency. It is not a list of 308 approved targets. Ten follow-up calls returned INVALID_ARGUMENT; these failures are recorded in the query log. No exact season 1, 2 or 4 Pakistan volume is established. Season 3's 1,600 estimate is from keyword expansion; its exact-detail follow-up failed.

Monthly volume is the provider's estimate, not October demand. Returned monthly history generally ends in August 2026. Season 4's new release can therefore be underrepresented. Blank volume is unavailable, not zero. US/UK data cannot measure worldwide English demand. LOW competition is not organic SEO difficulty. Variant estimates may overlap; do not sum them into unique audience totals.

## How episode searches are phrased

1. Season plus continuous episode number: `sultan muhammad fateh season 3 episode 70` = 1,300/month Pakistan; `...season 2 episode 49` = 480. These are primary candidates where the catalog maps to that broadcast episode.
2. Season-local numbering: `...season 3 episode 1` and `...season 3 ep 1` each show 880. Their estimates may overlap. TRT confirms season 3 starts with broadcast episode 50. Avoid making a duplicate episode-1 page; explain the verified alias on the existing episode-50 page.
3. No season: `sultan muhammad fateh episode 50` and `...episode 49` each show 110/month Pakistan. Episode 16 = 90. Use the same canonical page for verified equivalent wording.
4. Subtitle qualifier: `...season 3 episode 1 in urdu subtitles` = 110; `...season 2 episode 49 in urdu subtitles` = 50. Include subtitles naturally and state real per-episode language availability.
5. Dubbed wording: `mehmed fatih al sultani season 2 episode 1 urdu dubbed` = 1,600/month Pakistan, but the local catalog has no season-2 dubbed collection. This is an availability gap, not permission to label the subtitled page dubbed. Season-1 dubbed episode-1 query = 140; season-3 dubbed episode-1 = 90, also absent locally.
6. Platform names: Makki TV, Historical Point and Niazi Play appear in query variants. These are often navigational searches; do not imply affiliation or stuff competitor names into titles.
7. English wording: public results use `Mehmed Fetihler Sultani Episode 84 English Subtitles` and `Season 4 Episode 1 English Subtitles`. Measured English-subtitle expansions mostly return the different series `Mehmed Bir Cihan Fatihi`; exclude those from this drama's plan. Missing/small provider coverage does not establish no English audience.

## Numbering checks required

- Local season 1 subtitled catalog: 15 groups; dubbed: 8 groups. These are catalog counts, not proof of full live season availability.
- Local season 2: 36 groups, including an episode-1 record and two episode-49 records, one with Bolum 149 and another with Bolum 49. Do not normalize or merge from keyword data.
- Local season 3: 36 groups, even though the ordinary 50-83 broadcast range contains 34 numbers. Inspect duplicate/special records before claiming a season episode total.
- Local season 4: subtitled 3 groups, dubbed 1 group. Source labels map 1 to 84, 2 to 85, 3 to 86.
- Search suggestions include mismatched season numbers (e.g. season 1 episode 33; season 2 episode 70). A real query does not establish correct episode identity.

## Proposed page treatment

Season pages: regional alias + original title visibly, accurate season/edition labels, available episodes, and a short numbering explanation.
Episode pages: identify one verified episode, show both subtitle languages when present, preserve URLs, and explain verified broadcast/local aliases in visible content. Example: `Mehmed Episode 84 (Season 4 Episode 1) - Urdu & English Subtitles`. Include `Sultan Muhammad Fateh` naturally in the heading/intro rather than forcing every alias into the title.

Priority: fix production indexability when launch is intended; validate numbering and language availability; optimize the season-3 measured opportunities and current season-4 pages; then season-2 and season-1 evergreen entries. Keep English viewers served throughout.
